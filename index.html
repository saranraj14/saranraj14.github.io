Maturity projection appears here after parsing.
Rows are grouped by maturity date (soonest first) — lots that pay out on the same date are combined, since that's when you'll actually receive the money, even if they were bought separately at different prices. Projection method: since these bonds are held to maturity (not marked-to-market or sold early), the expected maturity value for each lot is estimated as Invested Amount × (1 + Expected Return % × Tenure in years) — a simple, non-compounded annualized return over the full holding period, then summed across the lots sharing that maturity date. This is an estimate based on the stated "Expected Returns" figure, not a guaranteed payout — the bond's actual terms (coupon schedule, call/put options, issuer default risk) determine the real amount received at maturity.
